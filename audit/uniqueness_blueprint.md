# Bhuntu 300-Page Uniqueness Blueprint

## Quality contract

Every registered route must have a route-specific title, a route-specific implementation or a deliberately distinct legacy room, a meaningful Abu-to-Samjhana message, a real-memory or future-plan reference, a visible gift or surprise, and a different primary interaction idea from adjacent pages. Shared navigation, the password gate, the footer, audio controls, and the global personal-gift layer are allowed to be shared infrastructure; the page experience itself must not feel like a renamed copy.

The active sequence contains **263 pages** from the **311 registered routes**. The 48 registered routes outside the sequence remain directly available for compatibility, but they are not allowed to introduce roulette, wheel, scratch-card, or generic randomized systems into the curated birthday trail.

## Removal rules

No active sequence page may use roulette, wheel, spin-the-bottle, scratch-card, scratch-off, random fortune, generic random quote, one-button photo swapping, or a generic arcade catcher. If a route name or legacy component still contains one of those ideas, the implementation must be replaced or the route must be removed from the curated sequence. A button-only experience is not accepted unless the button is the final step of a larger narrative composition.

## Validation gates

The final pass must report the full route count, sequence count, missing imports, unknown navigation targets, runtime-risk identifiers, repeated primary implementations in the sequence, repeated interaction ideas in the sequence, forbidden mechanic routes, media counts, gift completeness, retained-game count, and production-build status. The public build must also be tested from a fresh browser state: password gate, correct `Bhuntu` entry, first sequential transition, at least one later transition, and the final-route guard.
